Privacy Policy

Apakix Games · Kvadro: Block Puzzle and other games by Apakix Games · Effective date: 21 November 2026

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the mobile games published by Apakix Games (developer: Harutyun Baghdasaryan, Armenia), including Kvadro: Block Puzzle (the “Game”). Contact: apakix.games@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect ourselves

We do not require registration and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress, settings, coins, player name, avatar and the optional birthday (day and month only) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads and measure their performance, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and information about ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Feedback and ideas

If you use the “Ideas & feedback” or “Report a problem” feature, your email app opens with a prepared message. Only if you choose to send it, we receive your email address, your message and the technical details shown in it (game version, mode, level, screen size and device model). We use this only to answer you and improve the Game, and we do not share it with third parties.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game may show local reminders (for example, about the daily challenge). They are created on your device; no data is sent to us.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer; you can ask us to delete them at any time by writing to apakix.games@gmail.com.

8. Security

We do not store your personal data on our servers. Data handled by Google is protected under Google’s security practices.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
